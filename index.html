How the numbers are worked out
Month
Pick the month at the top. Every figure on the page is for that month; new months are added as their data arrives.
Available stock
Month opening SOH + releases in the month − sales to date − blocked stock, never below 0. Nov 2026 opening = Oct opening + Oct released − Oct sales, negatives set to 0.
Blocked stock
Stock awaiting pickup, blocked and not available for sale, from the list you shared. BROOKEBOND TP LAAL 24X400G is counted with Asian Roll 24X400G.
Remaining target
Month target − sales to date.
Gap
MIN(0, available − remaining target), per SKU.
Excess
MAX(0, available − remaining target), per SKU. Shown as “Excess – focus”.
Net gap
Gap + excess.
Tons
UOS ÷ cases per ton (CPT) from your price file. SKUs without a CPT (target lines with no stock SKU, and BROOKEBOND TB KSA with CPT 0) count as 0 Tons.
Colours
Red marks issues: shortages, blocked stock, negative net gap, cover below 100% and achievement behind time gone. Green marks good results and opportunities: SKUs on target, excess stock to push, positive net gap, cover of 100% or more and achievement at or ahead of time gone. Time gone = days of sales loaded ÷ 30 (Nov 2026 now 8 ÷ 30 = 27%).
Cover
Available stock ÷ remaining target.
SAR values
UOS × the SKU's value per case on the basis you pick: BPTT (default), CIF or DPLC (LC per case, from the price file). Target lines with no stock SKU (HI 24X20 packs, Pukka Feel New and others) and Pukka After Dinner (not in the price file) have no value, so they count in UOS only.
Channel targets
From your Target by channel file (KA by customer, WS, RVS, OOH, VFM, Ecom, LSM, Convenience). Matcha DTTC, Inf After Meal and Inf Quiet Moments there are the SKU list’s Matcha 3P, Inf After Dinner and Inf Time to Chill. Unallocated = SKU target − channel targets (HI 24X20 packs).
Sales
From the daily sales report (DSR), invoice dates within the month only, net of returns. DSR channels: Key Accounts = KA, HTS is counted under RVS, Wholesale = WS, Supermarket = LSM, Convinience = Convenience. KA customers: Panda Retail = Panda, Aswaq Abdullah Al Othaim = Othaim, Saudi Hypermarkets (Carrefour) = Oth MT.
Left out
Lipton T&I HTS Cups, removed at your request.
